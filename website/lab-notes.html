Operational concepts

What the lab demonstrates

Virtualisation layer

Proxmox provides the compute layer for the lab, separating workloads into VMs with their own storage, network interfaces, and recovery paths.

Network segmentation

VLANs 10, 20, 30, and 40 separate web staging, home-control, infrastructure, and media services, with OPNsense controlling traffic between them.

Service operations

Internal DNS, monitoring, backups, deployment scripts, and restore tests make the lab easier to operate, verify, and recover when something changes.
