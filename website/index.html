Operational concepts

Virtualisation layer

Proxmox provides the compute layer for the lab, separating workloads into VMs with their own storage, network interfaces, and recovery paths.

Network segmentation

VLAN 10, VLAN 20, and VLAN 30 separate public-facing, home-control, and infrastructure services, with OPNsense controlling traffic between them.

Service operations

Internal DNS, monitoring, backups, deployment scripts, and restore tests make the lab easier to operate, verify, and recover when something changes.

Practice. Record. Iterate.

This is my personal infrastructure learning lab. I use it to practise administration tasks, understand how systems fit together, and keep a clear record of progress across servers, networking, firewall rules, deployment automation, and recovery planning.
